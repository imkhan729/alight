Step 2: Open Your Project

After launching Alight Motion, you'll need to open or create a project:

  1. Tap "New Project" to start a new video or "Open Project" to work on an existing one.

Step 3: Import Audio or Music File

Now it's time to add your audio file to the project:

  1. In the timeline, tap the "+" icon to add a new element.
  2. Select "Audio" from the options that appear.
  3. Choose between "Music" for background tracks or "Sound" for effects.
  4. Browse your device's storage to locate the audio file you want to import.
  5. Select the file and tap "Open" to import it into your project.

Supported audio formats include MP3, WAV, AAC, and M4A. For best results, use high-quality audio files with bitrates between 128-320 kbps.

Step 4: Sync and Adjust Audio Settings

Once your audio is imported, you'll need to sync it with your video and adjust settings:

  1. Drag the audio layer along the timeline to align it with your video content.
  2. Tap on the audio layer to access audio controls.
  3. Adjust volume using the slider to balance with other audio elements.
  4. Use the fade-in and fade-out options to create smooth audio transitions.
  5. Trim the audio clip by dragging the edges if needed.
  6. Apply audio effects like reverb, echo, or equalizer adjustments if desired.

Pro tip: Use visual cues in your video to precisely sync audio events like claps or visual effects with corresponding sounds.

Step 5: Export Your Project with Audio

The final step is exporting your project with the added audio:

  1. Tap the "Export" button in the top right corner.
  2. Select your desired resolution and quality settings. The Mod APK allows 4K export.
  3. Ensure the "Include Audio" option is enabled.
  4. Choose your export format (MP4 is recommended for most uses).

Common Issues and Fixes While Adding Audio

Even with a powerful tool like Alight Motion, you might encounter some audio-related issues. Here are common problems and their solutions:

Audio Not Playing

  • Check device volume: Ensure your device isn't muted and volume is turned up.
  • Unmute audio layer: Verify the audio layer isn't muted in the timeline.
  • Supported format: Confirm your audio file is in a supported format (MP3, WAV, AAC, M4A).
  • Restart app: Close and reopen Alight Motion to refresh the audio engine.

Audio Out of Sync

  • Manual adjustment: Drag the audio layer left or right along the timeline to align with visuals.
  • Split and trim: Use the split tool to cut audio at specific points and adjust sections individually.
  • Frame rate match: Ensure your project frame rate matches your video footage.

Audio Quality Issues

  • Source quality: Start with high-quality audio files (128-320 kbps for MP3).
  • Export settings: Use higher quality export settings for better audio retention.
  • Volume clipping: Adjust audio levels to prevent distortion during export.

Audio Not Exporting

  • Check export settings: Ensure "Include Audio" is enabled in export options.
  • Free space: Verify your device has sufficient storage space for export.
  • File permissions: Check that Alight Motion has permission to access audio files.

Tips for Better Audio Editing in Alight Motion

Take your audio editing skills to the next level with these professional tips:

  • Layer multiple audio tracks: Combine background music, voiceovers, and sound effects on separate layers for better control.
  • Use keyframes for volume automation: Create dynamic volume changes by adding keyframes and adjusting levels at different points.
  • Apply audio effects sparingly: Subtle reverb or EQ adjustments can enhance audio without overwhelming the mix.
  • Normalize audio levels: Keep consistent volume levels throughout your project for a professional sound.
  • Preview on different devices: Check how your audio sounds on various speakers and headphones before final export.
  • Use fade transitions: Smoothly introduce and remove audio with fade-in and fade-out effects.
  • Match audio to visual pacing: Adjust music tempo and sound effect timing to complement your video's rhythm.
